# Spirited Away (2001)

## Overview

*Spirited Away* is an animated fantasy film written and directed by **Hayao Miyazaki** and produced by ***Studio Ghibli***. It follows a ten-year-old girl who gets trapped in a world of spirits.

---

## Quick Facts

| Detail   | Info                  |
|----------|-----------------------|
| Director | Hayao Miyazaki        |
| Studio   | Studio Ghibli         |
| Released | 2001                  |
| Genre    | Fantasy, Adventure    |
| Music    | Joe Hisaishi          |

## Main Characters

1. **Chihiro** – the curious and brave young protagonist
2. **Haku** – a mysterious boy who helps her
3. **Yubaba** – the witch who runs the bathhouse
4. **No-Face** – a lonely, silent spirit

## Why I Love It

- Beautiful hand-drawn animation
- A story about growing up and finding courage
- A soundtrack that fits every scene
  - Gentle piano themes
  - Orchestral build-ups

## Themes

> Miyazaki explores greed, identity, and the importance of
> working hard and staying true to yourself.
